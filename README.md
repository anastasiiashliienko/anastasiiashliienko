### 🚀 About Me

I work at the intersection of design, technology, and content, creating digital products as a whole systems from user journey logic to software implementation.  
My design approach is grounded in architecture, ergonomics, and attentions to detail. I map out user flows, design adaptive and responsive interfaces, and create layouts that account for technical constraints and future development. I also integrate AI tools into my workflow for research, visual experimentation, and complex graphics work.  
My background in game development has shaped my understanding of interactive environments and interaction logic, and my experience with content and analytics in B2B markets has honed my ability to handle complex information structures.
